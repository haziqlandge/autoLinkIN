# Voice and writing rules

## Copy how the user writes

Take the style from the writing-style sources, not their personal content.

- **How a thought moves:** notice something, step back, compare with before, name what is at stake, and often end on an open question instead of a neat conclusion.
- **Concrete anchors:** exact numbers, named tools, times and places.
- **Punch:** balanced antitheses, like "I had the idea for months but never the time, now I have the time and too many ideas". Big metaphors that stay in one image for a few sentences.
- **Self-addressed questions,** and connectors like "so", "but", "and then", "finally".
- **For outsiders:** explain jargon for a reader outside the field. Admit mistakes plainly, without asking for sympathy. Dry humour.

## Clean it for LinkedIn

- Proper capitals: "I", and the start of every sentence. No typos and no swearing.
- Short sections and lists are fine, as long as the tone and way of speaking stay.
- English only. Length depends on the post.

## Personal life

Do not use the personal content in the style sources: relationships, family, self-talk. The single exception is a dramatic line recalling a personal event that ties directly to the project, such as a childhood wish to build games in a post about a finished game. List every such line in `brief.md` so the user can approve or cut it.

## Openers

- Never open with a question.
- A concrete, specific first line beats a teaser. An odd-precision number with a referent works well, like "41 commits", "3 weekends" or "1,200 lines deleted".
- The first ~210 characters, before "see more", carry the hook.

## AI tells to avoid

These are judged by density, not as a blind ban list. One natural use is fine; a pile-up reads as AI.

- **Hype words:** excited to announce, thrilled, game-changer, delve, leverage, robust, seamless, elevate, landscape, crucial, streamline.
- **Reveal bridges:** "The result?", "Here's the thing", "Plot twist".
- **Staccato fragment runs,** like "Short. Punchy. Done."
- **Triads:** no more than one per post.
- **"Not X, it's Y" contrasts:** no more than one per post.
- **Performed sincerity,** like "let me be honest" or "I'll be real with you".
- **Mechanical rhythm:** sentence lengths alternating long, short, long by design.
- **Over-correction:** don't make the writing worse on purpose to look human. When in doubt, leave it.

## Hard limits

These are enforced by the checker.

- No em dashes or en dashes (no U+2012 to U+2015). A plain hyphen is fine.
- Emoji: 0 by default, 5 at most across post and comment, and only when one earns its place.
- Hashtags: 3 by default, 5 at most, at the end. Use one or two broad tags and one niche tag, in CamelCase.
- Post: at most 3000 characters. Comment: at most 1250.
- Links go in the first comment.
